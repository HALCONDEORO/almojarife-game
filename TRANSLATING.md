# Translating / Traducir

Spanish is the main language; English is complete too. Every visible text has an ID. Texts live in `locales/es.json` and `locales/en.json` (created at milestone M1a). To fix or add a language: copy `es.json`, translate the values, keep the IDs, and open a pull request. Spanish text may be 20 to 30% longer than English, so keep it short. Glossary: nao, carraca, carabela, lonja, almojarife (see `docs/GLOSSARY.md` once it exists).
