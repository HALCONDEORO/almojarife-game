# Social classes

Versión en español: [es/classes.md](es/classes.md).

Three classes: poor, middle, rich. The rich share grows with city size. Tolerance is the highest price multiple a class still pays.

Tables: [generated/classes.md](generated/classes.md); consumption per good and class: [generated/goods.md](generated/goods.md). To fill: the source type of each value.

Historical reference for the split of meat by class (institutional): Sevilla 1481, about 0.22 kg of mammal meat per person per day for an average resident against about 0.8 for a local elite household (González Arce 2022, doi 10.12795/hid.2022.i49.8). Not yet reflected in the game.
