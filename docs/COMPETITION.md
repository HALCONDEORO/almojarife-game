## Is there a Patrician successor?
Patrician IV is still sold (2010) with a "Mixed" Steam score. A discussion thread asks for a successor: https://www.reddit.com/r/tycoon/comments/1rmpl5g/a_successor_to_the_patrician_series/ . No announced Patrician V was found in these searches.

## What the gaps look like (opinion, from the data above)

1. There are living rivals, so the honest claim is not "nothing exists". The market is small-indie crowded in 2025-2027: High Seas High Profits, Argosy, Mare Nostrum, Trade Empire, Sail the Seas.
2. Spanish: Patrician IV has no Spanish. Argosy lists English only. The Spanish-first, fully bilingual angle is open, though High Seas High Profits and Mare Nostrum already ship Spanish.
3. Free and open source: none of the Steam titles above is open source. A free, browser-playable, moddable trading game is a real gap.
4. Scope discipline wins: reviews are strongest for compact, finished games (High Seas High Profits $8.99, Merchant of the Skies).
5. Risk: Argosy (online, rivals, mayor career) is aiming at the same fantasy as our rank ladder. We must differ on openness, language, readable economy and web play.
