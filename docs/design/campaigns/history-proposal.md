# Campaign story options, 20 October 2026

Recommend three short historical-context campaigns anchored to1503, 1511 and 1519. They fit the existing Iberian map if limited to port logistics and coastal trade. They do not recreate overseas journeys. All player characters, contracts, cargo targets, deadlines and dialogue would be original fiction, labeled as such, within documented context.

1. Sevilla 1503, A house by the river. Context: creation of Casa de la Contratación in Sevilla to manage supplies, ships and trade. Player: fictional small merchant learning cargo, costs and reports by delivering coastal supplies. Do not claim player founded or ran the real institution. Sources 1/2.
2. Bilbao 1511, Wool and iron. Context: Bilbao's consulate and competition with Burgos around shipping wool/iron. Player: fictional trader choosing cargo/routes, maintaining cash against rivals. Burgos/Flanders represented only by contextual letters, not invented playable ports. Sources 5/6.
3. Sevilla 1519, Before the departure. Context: material preparation of Magellan's expedition, hulls, cargo and crew. Player: fictional supplier assembling cargo and repair work before a departure. Not sailing around the world on a map that cannot show it; exact orders and deadlines are gameplay, not historical testimony. Sources 3/4.

Progression could unlock logistics complexity rather than lock geography or rewrite historical outcomes. Names, fiction boundary and goals need owner review before canon. Need separate economic calibration and ES+EN writing. No third-party prose/art copied. No enslaved people as trade goods. Context notes should acknowledge coercion and unequal colonial power where relevant rather than imply a neutral or purely celebratory setting.
