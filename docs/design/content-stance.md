# Content stance (M0, issue #10)

- The game is about goods trade. People are never a trade good, a cargo or a reward.
- No slavery mechanic, no depiction of it as gameplay. If the setting mentions it, it is outside the player's actions.
- Violence is limited to ship combat and pirates in abstract form (no gore).
- Ports and peoples are shown with respect: no caricatures, no slurs, neutral naming.
- Religion and politics appear only as flavour (guilds, the Crown, port rights), never as a mechanic that favours a faith.
- Real people and trademarks are not used. Names are original or generic.
- Age target: general audience.
- Review: any new content that touches these points is raised in a pull request and approved by the owner.
