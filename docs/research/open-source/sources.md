## Limitations

The effort estimates and ranking are project-specific engineering judgments, not upstream promises. No copied third-party game code is included in this research PR. No general grant is inferred for third-party music, images, icons, dependencies or model files. A future integration must preserve exact file-level provenance and applicable notices.
