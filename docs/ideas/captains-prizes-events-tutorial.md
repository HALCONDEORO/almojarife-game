# Ideas: captains, prizes from pirates, regional events, short tutorials

## Captains
- Source: Patrician IV manual, "Captains": each captain has four areas of experience with five levels each; experience grows with time and can be raised by trainers found in taverns. Combat experience adds 2% damage per point.
- Our version: one captain per ship with 2 skills (navigation: faster voyages; seamanship: lower loss chance in an encounter), 3 levels, growing with voyages made. No hiring of people as goods or any trade in people; captains are staff the player hires at fair wages.
- Cost: small once pirates exist (D). Not before.

## Prizes from pirates
- Source: Patrician IV manual, "Capturing Ships": you can take a pirate ship only when you were attacked, the opponent is a pirate, and you sank all of their ships but one and reduced its cannons to 0.
- Our version: after an won encounter against pirates, the player may keep the pirate ship (resold to the shipyard at a lower price), once per encounter. No capture of civil ships.
- Cost: small after D.

## Regional events (shocks)
- Source: Port Royale 4 update 1.3 notes (Kalypso blog): the world is divided into 8 regions; an economic shock can hit one or several regions or one nation, for example by lowering production.
- Our version: 3 regions of our map (Atlantic, Strait, Mediterranean) and a short list of events (poor harvest, port closed by plague scare, new demand) that change production or demand for a few weeks. Events are seeded and logged so the sim stays reproducible.
- Risk: events can break the M1b gates (pinned prices, bankrupt AI). Any event type must pass the 1,000-year gates before it ships. Plague and blockade are outside 1.0 scope per ROADMAP; use only mild events.

## Short tutorials with small rewards
- Source: Port Royale 4 manual, "Starting a Game": 10 short tutorials, a small reward after each; free game mode has no guided tour.
- Our version: the five or six first-hour steps (buy, sail, sell, read stock indicator, buy a second ship, set a first route) as short missions with a small in-game coin reward. Helps the M2 exit test ("outside testers finish it unaided").
- Cost: small; text is ES/EN from the start.
